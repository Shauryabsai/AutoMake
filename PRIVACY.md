Privacy Policy for AutoMake
​Last Updated: November 2026
​AutoMake respects your privacy and is committed to protecting any data accessed through third-party integrations, including the Google Ads API.
​Information We Access:
When you connect your Google Ads account to AutoMake, the application accesses Google Keyword Planner data (such as keyword search volumes, historical metrics, and keyword suggestions) via OAuth 2.0 authorization.
​How We Use Your Data:
Google Ads API data is used exclusively to display keyword metrics and market analytics to you inside your local AutoMake workspace. API credentials and fetched keyword metrics are stored locally on your machine and are never transmitted to unauthorized third-party servers.
​Data Sharing and Disclosure:
AutoMake does not sell, rent, trade, or share your Google Ads account data or personal information with any third parties.
​Revoking Access:
You can revoke AutoMake's access to your Google account at any time through your Google Account Security Permissions at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
